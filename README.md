I build sports software for hockey, football, and basketball.

---

### [MultiSport Elo Lab](https://multisport-elo-lab.streamlit.app/)

Elo plus Monte Carlo for NHL, NFL, and NBA. Toggle home-ice/court/field advantage, margin of victory, and Elevation Edge, and optionally grid-search the parameter values. Choose a target season and a start year for ratings, then simulate the schedule and playoff bracket. Playoff odds, conference spirals, win distributions, calibration against Log5 and other baselines, and an Excel export.

### [Football Ground Attack Lab](https://elevation-edge-sports-data.github.io/football-ground-attack-lab/)

A football game on one HTML5 canvas, no dependencies. You control the offense or the defense. Practice is untimed and unscored; pick both calls before the snap. Game mode is timed and scored. Sliders for speed, offense, defense, break block, and break tackle. Separate controls for how many players are at each position, field width, game length, and whether the next play starts on the snap or automatically. Works with keyboard or gamepad. Five control profiles (v1-v4, v6): eight extra D-pad moves on a gamepad (v2), steer nearby teammates (v3), and two-stick steering of the ball carrier and teammates on Xbox or PlayStation pads (v4). 3D stadium and player models, uniforms, field surface, endzone and midfield paint. Freestyle camera and instant replay with saved clips.

### [Playoff Elimination Tracker](https://elevation-edge-sports-data.github.io/playoff-elimination-tracker/)

Full playoff history for NHL, NFL, and NBA. Season-specific logos, arena locations, 2D or 3D, boundaries, logo scale, and camera.

### [NBA Win Probability](https://github.com/elevation-edge-sports-data/nba-win-probability/blob/main/nba_home_win_probability.ipynb)

Home-win models on a single NBA season: Log5, logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost, with chronological validation and calibration diagnostics.

### [NHL Playoff Team Stats](https://elevation-edge-sports-data.github.io/nhl-playoff-team-stats/)

Regular-season and playoff history plus modern process stats (xGF%, CF%, FF%) for every NHL club.

### [Workout Lab 100](https://github.com/elevation-edge-sports-data/workout-lab-100) · in progress

A local fitness ledger and on-device movement tracker. No ads. Data stays on the device until you export it.
